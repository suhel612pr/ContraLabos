Frequently Asked

How are wages calculated?
From verified attendance × the worker's daily wage rate. See Wages & Payments.

How do I request a material purchase?
Go to Requests & Status → New Request → select Material.

Who approves payment requests?
Accountant marks for review; Contractor gives final approval.
